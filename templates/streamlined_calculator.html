Calculation Formula Overview

Step 1 - Calculate Ratio:
Ratio = Area of Substance ÷ Area of ISTD
Example: Area(AcylCarnitine 10:0) ÷ Area(LPC 18:1 d7)
Step 2 - Calculate NIST:
NIST Result = Substance Ratio ÷ NIST Standard Ratio
Example: 0.9987 ÷ 0.0951 (from Ratio-database)
Step 3 - Calculate Agilent:
Agilent = Ratio × Conc.(nM) × Response Factor × Coefficient
Example: 0.9987 × 90.029 × 1.00 × 500
